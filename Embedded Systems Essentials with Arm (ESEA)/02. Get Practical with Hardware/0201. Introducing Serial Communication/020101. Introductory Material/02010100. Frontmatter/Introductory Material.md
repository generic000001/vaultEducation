## Module 01 Introductory Material

Welcome to **Introducing Serial Communication**. Explore how microcontrollers exchange data with peripherals through synchronous SPI and asynchronous UART, and consider how their differences inform engineering choices.

**Think about:** Which nearby devices use embedded systems, and what information do their components exchange?

## Module 01 Introductory Material

Welcome to Module 01: **Introducing Serial Communication**.

In this module, you will explore how microcontrollers exchange data with peripherals. You will learn the basic ideas behind serial communication, examine synchronous SPI and asynchronous UART, and consider how their differences affect engineering choices.

As you begin, consider: **Which devices around you use embedded systems, and what information might they need to exchange with other components?**

This section prepares you to study serial communication. Continue with the hardware information, thinking point, intended learning outcomes, and initial assessment below.

Welcome to Embedded Systems Essentials with Arm: Get Practical with Hardware.
This course is an opportunity to explore the technology behind many of the devices we use every day. Embedded systems are often designed for a particular purpose and combine hardware and software to carry it out.

They are found in connected home appliances, biomedical devices, autonomous vehicles, and Internet of Things (IoT) applications. Their growing role in a connected world makes understanding how they work a valuable step into the future of computing.

As you begin, consider: **Which embedded systems do you use or encounter in your daily life, and what tasks do they perform?**

Welcome to Embedded Systems Essentials with Arm: Get Practical with Hardware.
This course will provide you with the practical skills and confidence to build your own embedded system. Upon completion of the course, you will have taken your next step into the exciting future of computing. 

What are embedded systems and why do they matter? Watch this video to learn all about what awaits you in this Arm course.

## Overview

Embedded systems are the fundamental building blocks of our connected world, and **Arm-based devices are an industry standard**.

This course develops the practical skills and confidence needed to build an embedded system using the **ST NUCLEO-F401RE development board**.

Embedded systems are used in areas such as:

- Connected home appliances
- Biomedical devices
- Autonomous vehicles
- Internet of Things (IoT) applications

These systems often rely on **real-time operating systems (RTOS)** to manage multiple digital and analogue inputs, alongside efficient **communication protocols** for transferring data between peripherals and a microcontroller.

---

## Key Topics

The course focuses on two important areas of embedded systems development:

### 1. Serial Communication

The course begins with the basic principles of **serial data communication**.

You will learn how to implement:

- **Synchronous serial communication**
- **Asynchronous serial communication**

These techniques allow data to be transferred between a microcontroller and its peripherals.

### 2. Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS)

The course then introduces the multitasking capabilities of a **real-time operating system**.

You will learn how an RTOS can be used to manage microcontroller peripherals concurrently using:

- **Threads**
- Other **RTOS capabilities**

This provides a way of coordinating multiple activities within an embedded system.

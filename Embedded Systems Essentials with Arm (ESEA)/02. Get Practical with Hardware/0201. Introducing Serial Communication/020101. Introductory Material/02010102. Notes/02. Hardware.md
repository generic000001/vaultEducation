## Development Board

### ST NUCLEO-F401RE

The central piece of hardware is the **STMicroelectronics NUCLEO-F401RE**, an STM32 Nucleo-64 development board built around the **STM32F401RE** microcontroller.

The board includes an integrated **ST-LINK debugger/programmer**, so a separate programming/debug probe is not required.

## Hardware at a Glance

```text
NUCLEO-F401RE
      │
      ├── Serial / digital interfacing
      │     ├── Jumper wires
      │     └── 8-bit shift register
      │
      ├── User interface
      │     ├── 20×4 LCD
      │     ├── Red LED
      │     ├── 2 × green LEDs
      │     ├── 4 × tactile switches
      │     └── 2 × 10 kΩ potentiometers
      │
      ├── Sensing
      │     └── DS1631 digital temperature sensor
      │
      ├── Audio
      │     └── Piezo transducer
      │
      ├── Passive components
      │     ├── 4 × 100 Ω resistors
      │     ├── 4 × 1 kΩ resistors
      │     └── 4 × 10 kΩ resistors
      │
      └── Development connection
            └── USB-A → Mini-B cable
```

## Key Takeaway

> The practical labs require more than just the NUCLEO-F401RE. The course's distributor BOM also includes LEDs, resistors, potentiometers, tactile switches, a shift register, LCD, digital temperature sensor, piezo transducer, jumper wires and a USB cable. These components are used to turn the Nucleo board into complete embedded-system prototypes throughout the course.
